What is included in a package?

Open a sample package to compare inclusions and exclusions. A real quotation should specify hotels, flights, meals, visa assistance, baggage and transfers.

Can I travel with elderly family members?

Discuss mobility, medical and accessibility needs with the operator before booking. Confirm wheelchair availability, assistance fees and actual hotel walking distances.

Are the hotels and prices confirmed?

No. This is a demonstration. Every date, price and hotel category is illustrative; there are no live seats or real bookings.

How would payments and cancellations work?

The live operator must publish its payment schedule, cancellation rules, refund timelines and change conditions. This sample does not collect payments.
